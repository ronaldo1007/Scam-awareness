Scam & Phishing Alert

Be cautious of unexpected messages asking for your password, personal info, or urgent "act now" offers — including fake prize, gift card, or "free rewards" links. When in doubt, don't click, and verify directly with the source.

Stay Alert: Spot the Scam

A quick reference on common phishing tactics and how to protect yourself.

🔗 Suspicious Links

Hover over links before clicking. Scam links often use misspelled domains (e.g. "paypa1.com") or odd URL shorteners.

⏰ False Urgency

"Act now or lose access!" is a classic pressure tactic to stop you from thinking it through.

🎁 Too Good to Be True

Free gift cards, prizes, or rewards that ask for your login or personal info first are almost always scams.

🔒 Never Share Passwords

No legitimate company or service will ever ask you to type your password into an email or random form.

✉️ Check the Sender

Spoofed emails often mimic real addresses closely. Check the full email address, not just the display name.

🧐 When in Doubt, Verify

Contact the company or person directly through a known, official channel — not the one in the suspicious message.
